Receiving products

Operations team

Receive a trade-in one product at a time in the intranet (app.rewake.fr): photos, serial number, make and model, measurements, condition, and accessories. Once each product is complete, it is sent to Airtable; finish by closing the reception.

Each manual’s images are stored in docs/manuals/images/<manual>/. Until a real screenshot is available, a grey placeholder shows its number and what it should depict. A screenshot may become outdated when the app changes; the image guide records its status and what needs to be recaptured.
