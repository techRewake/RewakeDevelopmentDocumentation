Scripts
=======

Registered in ``api/scripts/registry.py``; each runs on demand via ``POST
/internal/scripts/{name}/run`` (see :doc:`architecture`), and the two
scheduled ones also run automatically (``beat``).

- :doc:`update_product` -- pushes one Airtable Model to Shopify (on demand;
  the only one of the three that writes anything).
- :doc:`shopify_catalog_reconciliation` -- verifies Shopify reflects the
  sellable Airtable catalog (hourly; ported from the RewakeMonitor proof of
  concept). Replaces the older, separate ``verification_shopify`` script,
  which did the same *kind* of comparison and has been removed.
- :doc:`airtable_catalog_stats` -- catalog-shape statistics, independent of
  Shopify (daily, 20:42 Europe/Paris; ported from RewakeMonitor).
