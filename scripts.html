Scripts

Registered in api/scripts/registry.py; each runs on demand via POST /internal/scripts/{name}/run (see Architecture), and the two scheduled ones also run automatically (beat).

  • Update Product – pushes one Airtable Model to Shopify (on demand; the only one of the three that writes anything).

  • Shopify Catalog Reconciliation – verifies Shopify reflects the sellable Airtable catalog (hourly; ported from the RewakeMonitor proof of concept). Replaces the older, separate verification_shopify script, which did the same kind of comparison and has been removed.

  • Airtable Catalog Stats – catalog-shape statistics, independent of Shopify (daily, 20:42 Europe/Paris; ported from RewakeMonitor).
